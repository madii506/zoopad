06 — FAQ

Straight answers.

Who holds the fund wallets?

ZOOPAD does, one wallet per sanctuary. None of the sanctuaries publishes a fixed Solana address a coin could pay into, so the fees have to land somewhere before they are donated. That is why every wallet is public and every donation is posted with its receipt.

Why not send fees straight to the sanctuary?

Their crypto donation pages create a fresh deposit address for each donation. A coin needs one fixed address for its whole life, so the fund wallet holds the fees until each donation.

What does ZOOPAD take?

Nothing from the fees. Launching costs Solana network fees, plus your first buy if you add one.

How are cases chosen?

Only cases where an animal was killed and something people did or decided set it in motion, with sources you can open. No rumours, and nothing that hasn't been reported.

Why isn't the Yorkshire Wildlife Park tiger here?

Because it wasn't killed. On 10 October 2026 a man died after entering the tiger enclosure at Yorkshire Wildlife Park, and police said the tiger was uninjured (UNILAD, Manx Radio).

Are the sanctuaries involved?

No. They are listed because they publicly accept crypto donations. None is partnered with ZOOPAD, and a donation is not an endorsement.

What if a sanctuary doesn't take SOL?

Its card says so. The fund converts the SOL into a coin the sanctuary's page accepts before donating, and the swap transaction is posted with the donation.

Can I launch for an animal that isn't listed?

Yes. Pick "Another animal" in the launch form and tell their story. The fees still go to the sanctuary you choose.
